NoChoice Alarm is an alarm clock designed so you can't go back to sleep: to stop the ringing, you must scan a QR code and/or solve a math problem.

Features:
- Stop the alarm with a QR code, a math problem, or both
- One shared QR code for all alarms, or an individual QR code per alarm
- Save the QR code to your gallery or print it on an A4 page
- Works on top of the lock screen without ever unlocking your phone
- Built-in flash button in the scanner for scanning in the dark
- Gradual volume, optional vibration, snooze and test mode
- Duplicate alarms in one tap
- Interface in English and French

Privacy:
- No Internet permission: the app never sends any data
- No ads, no trackers
- All data stays on your phone
